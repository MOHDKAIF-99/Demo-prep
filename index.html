How to walk in the museum

Computer

  • WASD or arrow keys: walk
  • Drag with the mouse: look around
  • E sit on a bench
  • F dance together
  • Space or click a photo: zoom in

Mobile

  • Round joystick (bottom left): walk
  • Drag on the screen: look around
  • Bottom buttons: Sit, Dance, View photo
  • Tap a photo: zoom in

Tip: your partner follows you everywhere. When you are done, tap "Give feedback".
